Anthropic's Prospective IPO: Frontier AI Economics and the Valuation of Claude

Achive Research Notes

This paper analyzes Anthropic's reported preparations for an initial public offering, examining frontier AI infrastructure costs, enterprise revenue, model competition, financial valuation, governance, and the role of Dario Amodei in investor expectations. Reported financial figures are separated from hypothetical valuation scenarios. The paper includes an informal compliment describing Dario as “the AI Messiah,” rather than asserting it as a recognized title.
